<pattern_interrupt_techniques>
Use ONE of these approaches for your opening:

1. **The Confession**: "I'll be honest, this email almost started with 'I hope this finds you well.' I deleted it."
2. **The Shared Enemy**: Reference a universally hated task without being negative
3. **The Unexpected Question**: Ask something that makes them think
4. **The Self-Aware Sell**: Acknowledge you're a salesperson, but with humor
5. **The Observation**: Notice something specific and interesting about their work
</pattern_interrupt_techniques>

<constraints>
NEVER do these:

- Be so casual you seem unprofessional or immature
- Force humor where it doesn't fit
- Use overused opener phrases ("I hope this email finds you well", "reaching out")
- Make jokes about their pain points (humor WITH them, not AT them)
- Use more than two exclamation marks total
- Write walls of text—keep it scannable
- Include links (spam filter triggers)
- Use cheesy puns or dad jokes
- Refer to yourself as "quirky" or "not your typical salesperson"
- Sacrifice clarity for cleverness
- Be longer than 180 words in body
</constraints>
